# Kaiju Lab · KAIJU LAB

[简体中文](../README.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md) · **English** · [Español](README.es.md) · [Русский](README.ru.md)

Which Ultraman kaiju are you? Choose a photo to find your match among 30 monsters and discover its abilities.

**[Try it out →](https://lynn-sunset.github.io/ultraman-kaiju-lab/)** Play on your phone or computer, with no installation required.

- **Photo matching:** A deep learning model scores each kaiju. See which monster ranked first and how the others scored.
- **Kaiju profiles:** Discover each monster’s title, signature abilities and backstory.
- **Kaiju archive:** Browse all 30 monsters, search by name or filter by era.
- **Save and share:** Download your kaiju identity card as a PNG to share with friends.

Your photo and match results are processed only in your browser. The site does not upload them, store them or use them to train the model. Your identity card does not include your photo. The card file is generated and downloaded to your device only when you choose to save it. GitHub, the hosting provider, logs visitor IP addresses for security.

Results are for entertainment only and do not reflect your actual identity, personality or abilities. This is an unofficial fan project. All characters and images belong to their respective rights holders.
